# ADR-001: Name and visual identity

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-22

## Context

The project needed a short, memorable name and a look that makes price drops obvious at a glance, in light and dark mode.

## Decision

- Name **Gier** (Dutch for vulture): it circles over prices until they drop. Tagline: _Circles over your prices until they drop._
- Palette: paper `#F5F2EA`, ink `#16161A`, prey lime `#C8F169` for drops and deals, rise `#FFE3D3`/`#9A3412` for increases and warnings, sand `#ECE7DB`, border `#DDD5C4`, muted `#5C574E`.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque 800 for display and prices, Inter for UI text with tabular numbers.
- Logo: lime rounded square with a dashed orbit and a bird (`branding/`).

## Consequences

Drops and rises differ in colour, lightness, arrow direction and wording, so meaning never depends on colour alone.
